What to Expect

A Focused Defense Process

  1. Initial consultation: The firm listens to your concerns and reviews the basic facts.
  2. Case review: Available reports, notices, evidence, and court information are examined.
  3. Legal strategy: The attorney explains the available options and develops an appropriate defense approach.
  4. Representation: The firm communicates with the client and handles agreed-upon court and case responsibilities.

Important

Do Not Ignore a Criminal Charge

Court dates and legal deadlines can arrive quickly. Missing a court appearance or failing to respond to required notices may create additional consequences.

Do not send detailed or confidential case information through ordinary email before the firm confirms representation.
