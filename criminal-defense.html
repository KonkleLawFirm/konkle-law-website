Criminal Defense

A criminal accusation can affect nearly every part of your life.

Court dates, release conditions, driving restrictions, employment concerns, and damage to your reputation can begin before a case is resolved. Early legal guidance may help you understand your rights, preserve important information, and avoid preventable mistakes.

Konkle Law Firm handles criminal matters from pre-filing investigation and bond proceedings through negotiations, trial, appeals, probation matters, and post-conviction issues, subject to the facts, jurisdiction, and attorney review.

Important Steps

Protect yourself while the case is pending.

Do not miss court.

Calendar every court appearance and comply with bond, probation, and release conditions.

Preserve documents and communications.

Keep notices, citations, messages, photographs, video, receipts, and other potentially relevant information.

Be cautious about statements.

Statements to law enforcement, witnesses, alleged victims, employers, insurers, and social-media audiences may affect the matter.

Seek legal guidance promptly.

Deadlines and strategic decisions may arise before a formal charge, arraignment, or trial date.

Common Questions

Criminal-defense questions.

Should I speak with police before consulting an attorney?

The answer depends on the circumstances. A person facing questioning or investigation should consider obtaining legal advice before making statements or consenting to an interview.

What should I do if I have an upcoming court date?

Do not ignore it. Contact the firm promptly, provide the date and available paperwork, and continue complying with all existing court orders unless a court changes them.

Does the firm offer free consultations?

Yes. Free initial consultations are available by telephone, video, or in person by appointment, subject to conflicts and availability.

How are criminal-defense fees structured?

Depending on the matter, the firm may use flat fees, hourly fees, payment arrangements, or a combination. The written agreement controls the fee and scope of representation.
