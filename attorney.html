Personal Representation

Legal representation begins with understanding the client.

Keith A. Konkle is the attorney behind Konkle Law Firm, PLLC. His practice focuses on providing personal, approachable representation to people facing criminal accusations and individuals dealing with the consequences of serious injuries.

Every legal matter begins with a different history, concern, and objective. Keith works to understand the client’s circumstances, identify the important legal issues, and explain the available options in clear and straightforward language.

Clients communicate directly with the firm and receive guidance designed around the individual facts of their matter rather than a one-size-fits-all process.

The Konkle Approach

Serious preparation. Personal service.

Strong representation should combine careful legal work with clear communication and respect for the individual client.

01

Direct Communication

Clients deserve understandable information about their legal matter, available options, and upcoming steps.

02

Careful Preparation

The firm reviews the facts, records, evidence, deadlines, and legal issues that may affect the client’s position.

03

Straightforward Advice

Potential risks and legal options are explained clearly, without unnecessary confusion or unrealistic promises.

04

Compassionate Service

Legal matters can affect a person’s family, finances, freedom, health, and future. Those concerns deserve personal attention.

Practice Focus

Representation during critical moments.

Keith evaluates criminal-defense and personal-injury matters throughout Florida, with a primary focus on Naples, Collier County, Lee County, and Southwest Florida.

01

Criminal Defense

Protecting rights, freedom, and the future.

Criminal accusations can affect nearly every part of a person’s life. The firm provides representation from pre-filing investigations through court proceedings, trial, appeals, probation matters, and post-conviction issues.

  • DUI and traffic-related criminal matters
  • Misdemeanor and felony charges
  • Drug-related allegations
  • Domestic violence and battery
  • Theft and property-related offenses
  • Probation and injunction violations

02

Personal Injury

Helping injured clients understand the way forward.

A serious injury can create medical expenses, missed work, physical limitations, and uncertainty. The firm helps clients understand their legal options and pursue compensation available under Florida law.

  • Car, truck, and motorcycle accidents
  • Pedestrian and bicycle injuries
  • Rideshare accidents
  • Premises liability and falls
  • Catastrophic injuries
  • Wrongful-death matters
