Information You Provide

Konkle Law Firm may receive information that you voluntarily provide when you call, text, email, submit the website intake form, request a consultation, or otherwise communicate with the firm.

This information may include your name, telephone number, email address, county, practice area, preferred contact method, preferred consultation date, upcoming court date, and the general description you submit.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, complete financial-account numbers, passwords, full medical records, or other highly sensitive information through the general website form, ordinary email, or text message.

Automatically Collected Information

Website hosting, security, and analytics providers may automatically collect limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited, approximate location, error information, request timing, and interactions with website links and features.

Google Analytics and Cookies

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to help the firm understand how visitors find and use the website. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies or similar technologies to measure page views, traffic sources, device and browser information, general geographic information, and website interactions.

The website may record that a visitor selected a call link, text link, intake button, or successfully completed the intake form. The firm does not intentionally send the contents of intake fields, names, personal telephone numbers, email addresses, court dates, or case descriptions to Google Analytics.

The Google Analytics tag is configured to disable Google signals and advertising-personalization signals. Visitors may restrict or delete cookies through their browser settings. Google also provides a browser add-on for opting out of Google Analytics measurement .

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and consultation requests;
  • Conduct an initial review of a possible legal matter;
  • Check availability, jurisdiction, and potential conflicts;
  • Communicate about scheduling or next steps;
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website;
  • Comply with legal, ethical, and professional obligations;
  • Protect the firm, prospective clients, clients, and others.

Website and Service Providers

The firm may use third-party providers for website hosting, form processing, email, communications, security, analytics, and other business functions. Those providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services.

The website currently uses Netlify for hosting and form processing. Form submissions may be stored in Netlify’s systems and delivered by email to the firm’s designated recipients. The website also uses Google Analytics 4 for aggregated website-usage measurement.

Telephone, Text, and Email Communications

When you provide contact information and request a response, the firm may contact you by telephone, text message, or email using the information provided. Standard message and data rates may apply to text messages.

Ordinary email and text messaging may not be secure. Do not use them to transmit highly sensitive information or documents unless the firm provides appropriate instructions.

Information Security

The firm uses reasonable administrative and technical practices appropriate to its website and operations. No internet transmission, email service, form processor, device, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

No Attorney-Client Relationship

Submitting information through the website, calling, texting, emailing, or requesting a consultation does not create an attorney-client relationship. The firm should not be considered your attorney unless it accepts the matter and the required written agreement is completed.

Children’s Information

This website is not directed to children. A parent or guardian seeking assistance regarding a juvenile matter should contact the firm directly.

External Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Konkle Law Firm does not control and is not responsible for their content, security, availability, or privacy practices.
