General Information Only

The materials on this website are provided for general informational purposes only. They are not legal advice and should not be relied upon as a substitute for advice from a lawyer regarding a specific matter.

No Attorney-Client Relationship

Viewing the website, calling, texting, emailing, submitting an intake form, requesting a consultation, or requesting payment instructions does not create an attorney-client relationship.

An attorney-client relationship begins only after Konkle Law Firm accepts the matter and the required written representation agreement is completed.

Do Not Send Confidential or Highly Sensitive Information

Before representation is confirmed, do not send Social Security numbers, complete financial-account information, passwords, detailed medical records, privileged documents, or other highly sensitive information through the website, ordinary email, or text message.

Information sent before representation is established may be reviewed for intake and conflicts, but submission does not guarantee confidentiality to the same extent as an established attorney-client relationship.

No Guarantee or Prediction of Results

Every legal matter is different. No statement on this website is a promise, warranty, guarantee, or prediction of a particular result. Past results, when referenced in the future, do not guarantee or predict a similar outcome.

Jurisdiction, Conflicts, and Matter Availability

The firm evaluates matters throughout Florida, with a primary focus on Naples, Collier County, Lee County, and Southwest Florida. Representation depends on jurisdiction, court admission, conflicts, deadlines, availability, complexity, and attorney review.

Some federal, appellate, medical-malpractice, nursing-home, product-liability, catastrophic-injury, or other specialized matters may require association with or referral to additional counsel.

Fees, Costs, and Payment Terms

Criminal-defense fees may be structured as flat fees, hourly fees, payment arrangements, or a combination, depending on the matter. Accepted personal-injury matters may be handled on a contingency-fee basis.

The written agreement controls the fee, responsibility for costs, payment obligations, and scope of representation. Responsibility for costs in a personal-injury matter depends on that agreement.

Online payment options are currently being updated. Existing clients should contact the firm for instructions before submitting funds.

Deadlines and Emergencies

This website and its forms are not emergency services. Do not rely on a website submission, email, or text message to preserve a legal deadline, court date, filing period, appeal deadline, statute of limitations, or other time-sensitive right.

Call 911 for an immediate safety or medical emergency. Contact the appropriate court, agency, or qualified lawyer promptly when a legal deadline or court appearance may be approaching.

Communication and Response Times
