After an Accident

Steps That May Protect Your Claim

  1. Seek appropriate medical attention.
  2. Report the incident to the appropriate person or agency.
  3. Preserve photographs, video, records, receipts, and correspondence.
  4. Avoid posting detailed statements about the incident online.
  5. Keep copies of medical bills and records of missed work.
  6. Speak with an attorney about applicable deadlines and options.

Possible Damages

Losses May Extend Beyond Medical Bills

Depending on the circumstances, a personal injury claim may involve medical expenses, lost income, diminished earning ability, property damage, pain, disability, and other legally recognized losses.
