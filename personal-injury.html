Personal Injury

An accident can affect your health, finances, work, and family.

Medical treatment, insurance communications, lost income, physical limitations, and uncertainty may begin immediately after an injury. Timely legal guidance may help preserve evidence, identify deadlines, and protect the value of a claim.

Konkle Law Firm evaluates a broad range of injury matters. When a claim would benefit from additional resources or specialized experience, the firm may associate with or refer the matter to additional counsel when doing so may better serve the client.

After an Injury

Steps that may help protect important information.

Seek appropriate medical care.

Health and safety come first. Follow appropriate medical instructions and keep records of treatment and expenses.

Preserve evidence.

Keep photographs, video, witness information, reports, damaged property, receipts, and written communications.

Be careful with insurance statements.

Recorded statements, releases, medical authorizations, and settlement documents may affect a claim.

Avoid detailed social-media posts.

Public statements, photographs, and activity may be reviewed by insurers or opposing parties.

Track financial losses.

Preserve bills, mileage, receipts, missed-work information, and documentation of other accident-related losses.

Ask about deadlines.

Different claims may have different notice requirements and filing deadlines. Delays can affect available options.

Common Questions

Personal-injury questions.

Does the firm offer free consultations?

Yes. Free initial consultations are available by telephone, video, or in person by appointment, subject to conflicts and availability.

Are accepted injury cases handled on contingency?

Accepted personal-injury matters may be handled on a contingency-fee basis. The written agreement controls the fee, costs, responsibilities, and scope of representation.

Will I owe case costs if there is no recovery?

Responsibility for costs depends on the written fee agreement and the circumstances of the matter. The agreement should be reviewed carefully before representation begins.

Can the firm refer or co-counsel my case?

Yes. When a particular claim would benefit from additional resources or specialized experience, Konkle Law Firm may recommend association with or referral to other counsel.
